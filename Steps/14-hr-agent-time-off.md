## What the professor gets

- In the **Professor view** chat, with no tag and no extra screen, he writes: *“My grandmother passed away, I need this Thursday and Friday.”*
- HR answers in the chat with **one clean card**. Its lead line is “I'm sorry for your loss. Check it and submit.” The card opens with a shaded **summary slip** he can read in 3 seconds: **Bereavement** (the absence name), the reason under it (**Grandparent/Great Grandparent**, chosen from “my grandmother”), the dates and length in words (“Thu, Nov 5 – Fri, Nov 6 · 2 days”), and what is left (“This doesn't use your vacation or sick leave.” or one big number such as “13 days of sick leave left after this” with one slim bar). Below the slip is the form.
- Every field is editable right in the card. There are no “Change” links and no extra pages. Required fields have a red star. The form is: **Kind of leave** (two autocomplete dropdowns side by side: Absence name, and Reason, which lists only that name's reasons), **Dates and hours** (first day, “to”, last day, hours), a **Note for your manager** (optional) with a small ⓘ for the privacy notice, then Submit request and Cancel. There is no relationship field: for a death the reason *is* the relationship. Privacy and proof notices are ⓘ icons, not text blocks.
- “I need Friday off” gives a card with the **Absence name dropdown empty and highlighted**, and HR's lead line asks what kind of time off it is. He can answer in the chat (“it's a vacation day”) or pick it in the card. Both work. A name with several reasons (Sick Leave, Bereavement) never gets a guessed reason: the Reason dropdown stays empty and highlighted until he or his words choose one.
- Part days come from the hours, with no extra field: for a 3-day request, typing 20 hours means “first day partial, rest full”, typing 6 means “6 hours each day”. When both fit and his words don't say, HR asks in the chat which one he means.
- “What's my sick balance?” and “Show my time off requests” are answered **in the chat**, not on the card.
- **Submit request** sends it. HR replies in the chat: “Sent to your manager for a decision.” with the request number. That is the end of the flow. There is no manager agent.
- “I'm sick, cancel Friday's class” is unclear, so the app asks: “Should I file this as time off with HR, tell the class, or both?” Nothing is posted or filed until he answers.
- A normal class message (“Read chapter 4 before Friday”) is posted exactly as before.
- A student can never reach HR.

## 2. The time-off form (`src/forms.js`, still dependency-free)

Add the workflow `WORKFLOWS['staff-time-off']` (skill id `staff-time-off`) from the CHRS page “Requesting/Entering Absences”.

**Concrete specification**
- **The absence list lives in `src/absence-types.js`** (no imports; the browser loads the same file from `/shared/absence-types.js`, a route in the UI server). `ABSENCE_TYPES` is the owner's real list: 51 rows `{ type, name, code, reason }` for 34 absence names, copied verbatim from `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-absence-types.psv` (his “Absence Type and Reasons.xlsx”; a test compares row by row). A name repeats because it has several reasons. Exports: `absenceNames`, `reasonsOf(type)`, `isPair(type, code)`, `nameOf(type)`, `defaultReason(type)`, `titleOf(type, code)` (title plus subtitle; the reason is the subtitle unless it repeats the name or is “None”), `CREDIT_OF_TYPE = { VAC: 'vacation', SCK: 'sick', PHL: 'personalHoliday' }`, `CREDIT_LABEL`, `PROOF` (Jury Duty: summons or certificate of service; Witness: subpoena; Military leave: orders), `NOTE_PRIVACY` (“Your manager, your timekeeper and Payroll Services can read this note. For anything confidential, talk to your manager directly.”), `HOLIDAYS`, `STANDARD_DAY` 8, `MAX_SPAN_DAYS` 400, `shortDate`, `workdays`, `spreadOf`, `daysText`, `whenText`. Every other name uses no balance. `defaultReason`: a name with one reason, or a reason worded exactly like the name (Vacation, Dock, Other), fills it in; Bereavement and Sick Leave never get a guessed reason.
- `spreadOf(days, hours, wordsMode)` turns the hours into a part-day shape, for N working days and H hours: `invalid` when H is 0 or more than 8N; H = 8N → `all_full`; N = 1 → `one_day`; otherwise “first day partial, rest full” (`start_day_only`) fits when 8(N−1) < H < 8N and “the same hours each day” (`all_days`, only when H/N is a whole number of quarter hours) fits when H/N < 8. Only one fits → use it. Both fit → the professor's words decide; if they do not, `ask` (HR asks in the chat, never guesses). `daysText(hours)` shows days in quarters (¼ ½ ¾) and anything that is not a whole quarter day in hours (so a 3-second read is never “0.37 days”). `whenText` writes the dates and length in words. `shortDate('2026-11-05')` is `Thu, Nov 5` (fixed English); `shortDate` and `workdays` accept only real calendar dates (Feb 30 gives `''` / `[]`).
- `buildTimeOffForm`: a Column. Row **Kind of leave**: two **ChoicePickers** with `filterable: true` and `displayStyle: 'chips'` (single choice, drawn by our CSS and a small open/close script as a closed dropdown that opens into a search box and a list; the agent still sends a standard ChoicePicker, no custom component) at `/absenceType` (names) and `/absenceReason` (codes of the chosen name only, built by the exported `reasonPicker(type)`). Row **Dates and hours**: DateTimeInput `beginDate`, “to”, `endDate` (date only, no min/max), TextField `hours`. Then `note` (“Note for your manager”, optional) and **Submit request** / **Cancel**. Submit's check needs both pickers non-empty. The date and hours labels end with “ *” (“Begin date *”, “End date *”, “Hours *”); the pickers are labelled “Absence name” and “Reason” without it (the browser's CSS adds a red “*” after the picker labels). When the name changes in the browser, send `updateComponents` with `reasonPicker(newType)` and keep `/absenceReason` if `isPair(newType, reason)`, otherwise set it to `[defaultReason(newType)]` or `[]` (the same rule as HR's merge). Data paths: `/absenceType`, `/absenceReason`, `/beginDate`, `/endDate`, `/hours`, `/note`, plus the usual `/ui` flags. The approved mockup is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-hr-card-redesign.html`. Only the fixed-form component and function set (Column, Row, Text, ChoicePicker, DateTimeInput, TextField, Button; required/regex/length/and/not/or).
- `validateTimeOff` (also used by `validateFinal('staff-time-off', …)`): rejects unknown keys; the name must be on the list and name + reason must be a real pair; real `YYYY-MM-DD` dates with begin <= end; hours more than 0 and at most 24 per day of the range, and the hours must fit the dates (`These hours don’t fit the dates.`); `note` at most 1000 characters (trimmed, and must be text). Returns `{ ok, errors }`.
- `checkTimeOffValues(values)`: the open card's current values sent with a chat follow-up (half-filled is normal). Not an object, or unknown keys → `{ ok: false }` (the whole message is refused). Otherwise each field is checked with the same field rules as `validateTimeOff`; a valid non-empty one is kept (normalised to text), an invalid one is dropped. A reason that does not belong to the chosen name (or has no name) is dropped. Returns `{ ok, values, errors }`.
- Checks run in three places like the other forms: the card, the UI server, and HR (authoritative).

## 3. The store: dates, hours, Forecast (`src/hr-store.js`)

HR's store owns the numbers. The model and the browser never supply them.

**Concrete specification**
- Fictional staff `staff-amir`, “Professor Amir”, a **department chair on a 12-month appointment** (so he earns vacation; your words: “12-month faculty, department chair with vacation”): standard day 8 hours; Vacation 96 h (earns 16 h a month), Sick 120 h (8 h a month, no limit), Personal Holiday 8 h (one day a calendar year) — rates from CSU sources (CFA contract Art. 24; CSU benefits summary). Sample campus holidays: 2026-11-11, 2026-11-26, 2026-11-27, 2026-12-25.
- `plan(staffId, { absenceType, absenceReason, beginDate, endDate, partial })` is the **Forecast**. The type must exist (validate it before planning) and the pair must be real. Workdays are Monday to Friday minus the holidays. Hours = workdays × standard day, or a partial: `start_day_only` `{ hours }` shortens the first day only, `all_days` `{ hours }` gives the same hours each day (hours more than 0 and less than the standard day, else `bad_hours`, “less than 8”). An absence can't begin or end on a holiday, must include a workday, can cover at most **400 days** (`too_long`), and can't use more credit hours than the balance (`not_enough`, with the exact text “Not enough sick leave: 16 hours needed, 8 left.”). Names with no credit pass.
- `submit(staffId, values, operationId)` is synchronous check-and-write (no `await` between the check and the write). It is idempotent by operation ID: same values return the stored receipt; the same ID with different values is refused. Operations are kept in a `Map`. The receipt is `{ requestId: 'TO-…', absenceType, absenceReason, absenceName, reasonText, beginDate, endDate, hours, status: 'sent_to_manager' }`. Nothing is deducted. `balances(staffId)` and `requests(staffId)` (newest first, receipt fields only) feed the chat answers.
- `shortDate('2026-11-05')` is `Thu, Nov 5` (fixed English, not locale based).

## 4. Reading the professor's words (`src/hr-intent.js`, `src/hr-model.js`)

The model proposes, code decides.

**Concrete specification**
- `readDates(text, now)` and the partial-day reader are **code**, never the model. They read “this Thursday and Friday”, “next Monday”, “today/tomorrow/yesterday”, “Nov 5”, “Nov 5–7”, “11/5”, and a month with a list of days: “Nov 5th and 6th” → Nov 5–6, “Nov 5, 6 and 7” → Nov 5–7 (each listed day must be the day after the one before; “Nov 5 and 7” is asked, never widened; a joiner and a number left over after a month date, e.g. “Nov 5th and the 6th”, refuses the whole reading). Anything it can't read with confidence returns **null**, never a wrong date (see the problems below).
- `chooseAbsence({ text })` (`hr-model.js`): live is one structured call returning `{ pair, quote, note }`, where `pair` is one of the 51 `TYPE:CODE` keys or empty (the system text lists each as `TYPE:CODE = Name — Reason`); mock is a labelled keyword heuristic. Then `verify()` keeps the result only if the type exists **and** the quote is really in the professor's words (case-insensitive). **The reason for a death or a sick relative is chosen by code, not the model:** `reasonForRelative(text)` maps the relative in his words (grandmother → `GGP` Grandparent/Great Grandparent, and so on; a fixed list with plurals, grandma/grandpa, great-grand…, step… and in-law forms, the longest word wins, “son” is not read inside “son-in-law”) to a Bereavement reason; two relatives with different codes give no reason, so HR asks. Sick Leave becomes Family Care only when the relative is the one who is ill or cared for **by the speaker** (`familyCare`), never because a relative is merely mentioned (“my mother says I should rest”). A relative who is someone else's (“my colleague's mother passed away”) never sets a reason. No reason given gives an **empty** name, never Vacation. A model failure gives an empty name and a sanitized error code.
- `readRequest` returns `{ prefill: { absenceType, absenceReason, beginDate, endDate }, partial, startAt, side, quote, note, missing, datesUnclear? }`, with `missing` a subset of `absenceType | absenceReason | dates`. It is ledgered by `contextId|messageId`, so a repeat makes no second model call.
- **Part days over several days** (`startDayOnly`): “leaving at noon Thursday, out Friday” is a first-day partial only when exactly one clause carries an hour signal, that clause names exactly one day (the first day), every other clause naming a day says he is away (not negated), the days are workdays with no gap, and no correction word appears. “4 hours each day Mon to Wed next week” is `all_days` (hours-per-day phrases need a day-range cue; “2 hours a day for meetings” is not leave). “Next week” belongs to the clause it sits in. Someone else's absence (“my colleague is out Friday”) never extends his range; a conditional extra day (“and Monday if the test is bad”) is not added. Noon counts as 5 hours (a partial day ends at 17:00).
- `readRequest` is ledgered by `contextId|messageId`, so a repeat makes no second model call.

## 5. HR's chat behavior (`src/hr-agent.js`)

**Concrete specification**
- Staff check first: `hubLink.verifiedStaff(metadata, contextId)`. A missing or invalid signature, or a staff id HR doesn't know, gets the text “HR serves university staff only.” (`metadata.hr.outcome = 'refused'`). Never a student answer.
- A chat message becomes a card (an A2A task in `INPUT_REQUIRED`) with `hrCard: { title, subtitle, when, why, intro, question, problem, missing, credit, proof, balances, spread, startAt, side }`. `credit` is `{ type, label, now, after }` from the Forecast (or null); `proof` is the `PROOF` text for Jury Duty, Witness and Military leave; `spread` is `spreadOf(…)` (null while dates are missing). Lead lines (approved mockup): no name yet → the lead itself asks for it; otherwise “I'm sorry for your loss. Check it and submit.” for Bereavement, Sick Leave with the Bereavement reason, and the death reasons of Other; “Feel better soon. Check it and submit.” for Sick Personal only; else “Check it and submit.” The why line is “Picked from what you wrote: “<quote>”.” and shows only while the name is still the one his words chose. The receipt text is “Sent to your manager for a decision. Request TO-…: Sick Leave (Sick Personal), Fri, Nov 6, 8 hours.” (the reason in brackets only when it differs from the name).
- **Missing fields:** the card shows the empty field highlighted and the chat text ends with one short question: name → asked in the lead line (“What kind of time off is this — for example vacation, sick, or a personal holiday?”), reason → “What is the reason?” for a name with several, dates → “Which day or days?”, part day that could be read two ways → “Is that 4 hours on Thu, Nov 5 and the rest in full, or 6 hours each day?”. Nothing is filed until it is answered; Submit with an unanswered spread is refused with that same question. The holiday check comes before the spread question, so a range ending on a holiday is refused as such first.
- **Follow-up while a card is open** (same conversation): first the card's **current values** (`metadata.formValues`, when the Professor sent them — checked again with `checkTimeOffValues`; when sent they ARE the card, so a field cleared there is empty), then the new words: non-empty values from the words replace those. Dates replace together. After the name is settled, the reason is the first of these that is a real pair for it: the words' reason (only when the words named the type), a relative in the words (Bereavement, or Family Care for a sick relative), the card's reason, `defaultReason`, else empty — so a name change that leaves a foreign reason on the card clears it. **Card-typed hours are kept when the dates move on the card** (typing 4 hours then changing the dates keeps 4 hours, re-read through `spreadOf`); the words' own partial day wins; a spread the words chose counts only for those exact dates and hours (`spreadAnswer`: “the first day only” → `start_day_only`, “each day” / “same hours each day” → `all_days`; anything else keeps asking), and hours retyped on the card drop it. The “picked from what you wrote” quote stays only while the name is still the one those words chose. The card is re-issued with `supersedes`, and the old surface is removed with the standard `deleteSurface`.
- **Submit:** re-verify the staff member against the card's owner, run `validateFinal`, run `spreadOf` on the card's hours (full-day total → no partial; `one_day` / `start_day_only` → a first-day partial; `all_days` → the same hours each day; `ask` or `invalid` → refused with a plain message), then `store.submit`. Result `accepted` with “Sent to your manager for a decision.” and the receipt; refusals `rejected` with the reason and the card stays open. Cancel closes the card.
- **Balances and requests in the chat** (`asksBalance`, `asksRequests` in `hr-intent.js`; answered by HR as plain text with no card, no model call, an open card untouched; the Professor desk sends them to HR without the classifier): only the professor's **own** leave — first person (my / do I have / I have … left) AND a leave word (vacation, sick leave/days/hours, personal holiday, PTO, leave balance, time off) AND a quantity form (balance, how much/many, remaining, left). Vetoes: a student tag, a first-person leave-request cue, any day or date word (including today, this/next week), and class words (semester, students, class, homework, exam, grading, lab, assignment, submit). So “what's my sick balance?” is answered, while “how much vacation do I have left before Friday?” and “how many students are left in my class?” are not. Requests need “my (time off|leave|absence) requests” or “what time off have I filed”.
- HR server logs never contain the chat text; the reason code and note are redacted.

## 7. The Professor's staff desk (`src/professor-staff.js`, `src/professor-agent.js`, `src/professor-model.js`)

## 8. Privacy: leave words never reach students

The professor's own HR messages are private. The code enforces it; the model's good behavior is not enough.
