## What the professor gets

- In the **Professor view** chat, with no tag and no extra screen, he writes: *“My grandmother passed away, I need this Thursday and Friday.”*
- HR answers in the chat with **one clean card**: “I'm sorry for your loss. Here's your request. Check it and submit.” The headline is **Bereavement/Funeral**, with the dates and hours, and the reason it chose that name quoted from his own words. The relationship is filled in (“grandmother”).
- Every field is editable right in the card. There are no “Change” links and no extra pages. Required fields have a red star. A bar shows the leave balance that matters (for example Sick hours 120 now, 115 after), or says the request doesn't use his vacation or sick hours.

- “I'm sick, cancel Friday's class” is unclear, so the app asks: “Should I file this as time off with HR, tell the class, or both?” Nothing is posted or filed until he answers.
- A normal class message (“Read chapter 4 before Friday”) is posted exactly as before.
- A student can never reach HR.

## 2. The time-off form (`src/forms.js`, still dependency-free)

Add the workflow `WORKFLOWS['staff-time-off']` (skill id `staff-time-off`) from the CHRS page “Requesting/Entering Absences”.

**Concrete specification**
- `ABSENCE_NAMES` (en dash, labelled “sample list from CSU CHRS”): Vacation, Personal Holiday, Sick – Self, Sick – Family Care, Sick – Bereavement, Bereavement/Funeral, Jury Duty, Professional Development, Military Leave, Dock.
- `CREDIT_OF`: Vacation → vacation, Personal Holiday → personalHoliday, the three Sick names → sick. The others use no credits. `RELATIONSHIP_REQUIRED`: Bereavement/Funeral, Sick – Family Care, Sick – Bereavement.
- `buildTimeOffForm`: a Column with one field per Row: **ChoicePicker** `absenceName` (chips; see the problems below), DateTimeInput `beginDate` and `endDate` (date only, no min/max), TextField `hours`, `relationship` (**always on the card**: its label is bound to `/ui/relationshipLabel` — “Relationship *” when the name needs one, else “Relationship (bereavement or family care)”; its check passes when it is filled or `/ui/relationshipNeeded` is empty) and `note` (“Note for manager”, optional), then **Submit request** and **Cancel**. Required labels end with “ *”. Data paths: `/absenceName`, `/beginDate`, `/endDate`, `/hours`, `/relationship`, `/note`, plus the usual `/ui` flags and `/ui/relationshipNeeded` (`'yes'` or `''`) and `/ui/relationshipLabel`. Export `RELATIONSHIP_LABEL = { required, optional }`. The card's approved mockup is kept at `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hr-card-mockup.html`. Only the fixed-form component and function set (Column, Row, Text, ChoicePicker, DateTimeInput, TextField, Button; required/regex/length/not/or).
- `validateTimeOff` (also used by `validateFinal('staff-time-off', …)`): rejects unknown keys; the name must be on the list; real `YYYY-MM-DD` dates with begin <= end; hours more than 0 and at most 24 per day of the range; relationship required where applicable; `relationship` at most 100 and `note` at most 1000 characters (trimmed, and must be text). Returns `{ ok, errors }`.
- `checkTimeOffValues(values)`: the open card's current values sent with a chat follow-up (half-filled is normal). Not an object, or unknown keys → `{ ok: false }` (the whole message is refused). Otherwise each field is checked with the same field rules as `validateTimeOff`; a valid non-empty one is kept (normalised to text), an invalid one is dropped. Returns `{ ok, values, errors }`.
- Checks run in three places like the other forms: the card, the UI server, and HR (authoritative).

## 3. The store: dates, hours, Forecast (`src/hr-store.js`)

HR's store owns the numbers. The model and the browser never supply them.

**Concrete specification**
- Fictional staff `staff-amir`, “Professor Amir”: standard day 8 hours; Vacation 96 h, Sick 120 h, Personal Holiday 8 h. Sample campus holidays: 2026-11-11, 2026-11-26, 2026-11-27, 2026-12-25.
- `plan(staffId, { absenceName, beginDate, endDate, partial })` is the **Forecast**. Workdays are Monday to Friday minus the holidays. Hours = workdays × standard day, or a `start_day_only` partial that shortens the first day only (hours more than 0, at most the standard day). An absence can't begin or end on a holiday, must include a workday, can cover at most **400 days** (`too_long`), and can't use more credit hours than the balance (`not_enough`, with the exact text “Not enough vacation hours: 104 needed, 96 left.”). Absences with no credit pass.
- `submit(staffId, values, operationId)` is synchronous check-and-write (no `await` between the check and the write). It is idempotent by operation ID: same values return the stored receipt; the same ID with different values is refused. Operations are kept in a `Map`. The receipt is `{ requestId: 'TO-…', absenceName, beginDate, endDate, hours, status: 'sent_to_manager' }`. Nothing is deducted.
- `shortDate('2026-11-05')` is `Thu, Nov 5` (fixed English, not locale based).

## 4. Reading the professor's words (`src/hr-intent.js`, `src/hr-model.js`)

The model proposes, code decides.

**Concrete specification**
- `readDates(text, now)` and the partial-day reader are **code**, never the model. They read “this Thursday and Friday”, “next Monday”, “today/tomorrow/yesterday”, “Nov 5”, “Nov 5–7”, “11/5”, and a month with a list of days: “Nov 5th and 6th” → Nov 5–6, “Nov 5, 6 and 7” → Nov 5–7 (each listed day must be the day after the one before; “Nov 5 and 7” is asked, never widened; a joiner and a number left over after a month date, e.g. “Nov 5th and the 6th”, refuses the whole reading). Anything it can't read with confidence returns **null**, never a wrong date (see the problems below).
- `chooseAbsence({ text })` (`hr-model.js`): live is one structured call returning `{ absence_name, quote, relationship, note }`; mock is a labelled keyword heuristic. Then `verify()` keeps the result only if the name is on `ABSENCE_NAMES` **and** the quote is really in the professor's words (case-insensitive). The relationship must also appear in his words. No reason given gives an **empty** name, never Vacation. A model failure gives an empty name and a sanitized error code. `readRelationship(text)` reads a family word from a fixed list when an open card still needs one.
- `readRequest` is ledgered by `contextId|messageId`, so a repeat makes no second model call.

## 5. HR's chat behavior (`src/hr-agent.js`)

**Concrete specification**
- Staff check first: `hubLink.verifiedStaff(metadata, contextId)`. A missing or invalid signature, or a staff id HR doesn't know, gets the text “HR serves university staff only.” (`metadata.hr.outcome = 'refused'`). Never a student answer.
- A chat message becomes a card (an A2A task in `INPUT_REQUIRED`) with `hrCard: { title, when, why, intro, balance, note, missing, question, problem }`. Fixed copy: intro “Here's your request. Check it and submit.”, prefix “I'm sorry for your loss. ” for Bereavement/Funeral and Sick – Bereavement, prefix “Feel better soon. ” for Sick – Self. The why line is “Chosen because you said “<quote>”.” The note “This doesn't use your vacation or sick hours.” appears only when a name is chosen and uses no credits.
- **Missing fields:** the card shows the empty field highlighted and the chat text ends with one short question: name → “What kind of time off is this — for example vacation, sick, or a personal holiday?”, dates → “Which day or days?”, relationship → “Who in your family is it for?”.
- **Follow-up while a card is open** (same conversation): first the card's **current values** (`metadata.formValues`, when the Professor sent them — checked again with `checkTimeOffValues`; when sent they ARE the card, so a field cleared there is empty), then the new words: non-empty values from the words replace those. Dates replace together. The relationship is dropped when the words change the name to one that doesn't need it. Whenever the **current** name (from the card or the words) needs a relationship and none is filled, it is read from these words (`readRelationship`). The card's hours (if the dates are unchanged) become a first-day partial through the same mapping as Submit; the words' own partial day wins. The “because you said” quote stays only while the name is still the one those words chose. The card is re-issued with `supersedes`, and the old surface is removed with the standard `deleteSurface`.
- **Submit:** re-verify the staff member against the card's owner, run `validateFinal`, map the card's hours to the store (equal to the full-day total means no partial; less by under one standard day means a first-day partial; anything else is refused with a plain message), then `store.submit`. Result `accepted` with “Sent to your manager for a decision.” and the receipt; refusals `rejected` with the reason and the card stays open. Cancel closes the card.
- HR server logs never contain the chat text; relationship and note are redacted.

## 6. The Hub's staff path (`src/staff.js`, `src/hub-agent.js`)

**Concrete specification**
- Two typed A2A data parts, like Part 9's lesson publishes: `application/vnd.a2a-spike.staff-request+json` and `…staff-action+json`. Signed fields: request `['staff-request', operationId, conversationId]`; action `['staff-action', operationId, formRef, conversationId]`. `conversationId` is **required** on actions and must equal the conversation the card was issued in.
- The Hub verifies the sender with its registration key (`verifyAgentSignature`), maps the agent to a staff record from **its own config** (`staff: { professor: { staffId: 'staff-amir', name: 'Professor Amir' } }`), and forwards to the first ready staff destination (`decidedBy: 'staff'`, no router call). - `destinations.hr = { worker: 'hr', audience: 'staff', name: 'Human Resources' }`. Staff destinations are **not** on the Hub's public card, not in the student router's view, and `requestedCapability: 'hr'` from a student is rejected (`capability_rejected`). - A request may carry an optional `formValues` object (the open card's current values). The Hub checks it with `checkTimeOffValues` (a malformed one is refused), includes it in the dedupe hash, and passes the checked values to HR as `metadata.formValues` (never in telemetry: events say only `cardValues: true`).

- Topology and events: staff runs are named `staff-<operationId>` and carry only IDs, statuses and counts, never staff words (the Hub's allowlist; HR content only with `HUB_TELEMETRY_SHOW_CONTENT=1`).

## 7. The Professor's staff desk (`src/professor-staff.js`, `src/professor-agent.js`, `src/professor-model.js`)

## Avoid these known problems

- **Time off must never be posted to students.** A first design let the `@assistant` model see the professor's HR messages and draft a class post with “my grandmother passed away” in it. Hide staff entries from the model with the private marker and refuse drafts that reuse their distinctive words (`private_staff`). Code decides, not the prompt.
- **Don't read fractions as dates.** “1/2 day” and “3/4 of the day” are not January 2 or March 4. Skip fractions, and numbers followed by day/hour words.
- **Don't merge unrelated dates into one span.** “Thursday” in one sentence and “Nov 20” in another are not a range. Only join mentions that are joined by “and”, a comma or “to”.
- **A cross-month range never runs backwards.** “Dec 30 – 2” ends on Jan 2 (next month, next year if December), never on a day before the start.
- **A number followed by hours/a time is never a day.** “Nov 6 - 2 hrs” and “Nov 6, 4 hours” mean Nov 6 (a range end or leftover day must not be followed by hrs/hours/am/pm/a time).
- **Partial days follow one strict structural rule (Rule A) and fail safe.** Every hour cue in the message is a candidate. A clock time is a cue ONLY right after the professor's own leaving verb (leave/leaving/out/head out/going home … at T → 17:00 − T) or own arrival verb (coming in/be in/in/arriving … at T → T − 08:00), a day/date may sit between (“leaving Friday at 2pm”). “starting at T”, “from T” and “back at T” are not cues. Any clock time in the message that is not inside the one verb-tied cue BLOCKS: “meeting at 3pm”, “I'm off starting at 2pm Friday”, “Leaving at 2pm Friday, no wait, noon”, “I'll leave at 2pm Friday, the dentist moved it to noon” → null; “students leave at noon” blocks; “Out Friday, back at 2pm” stays a full day. Also cues: each “N hours/hrs/h” and “N-hour” (decimals, “.5”, “an/one hour” = 1), each “N hours early/late”, and morning/afternoon/half day ONLY when tied to being off/out/sick/leaving (“off the afternoon”, “out this morning”, “sick yesterday afternoon”, “afternoon off”, “half day”) — “I have class in the morning” is not a cue. Remove cues clearly not about the absence: 8 hours or more, over 24, “N hours notice”, “in N hours”, “2 hours of meetings”. A cue present but not readable as the absence's length blocks (an early/late phrase that isn't the professor's own — third party, negated, retracted, more than a day/time and short reason after it; “Room 4h”). Return a partial day ONLY when exactly one readable cue remains AND no correction/alternative marker appears anywhere (or, make it, otherwise, unless, instead, I mean, actually, perhaps, maybe, either, jk, nvm, never mind, scratch that, on second thought, cancel, no wait, correction — for every kind of cue), AND, after that one cue, no number or number word (digits, one…twelve, half, noon, midnight, “an hour”; a date such as “Nov 6” excepted) and no retraction or doubt word (sorry, moved, oops, make that, no, wait, earlier, later, possibly, maybe, might, probably — “Leaving at 2pm Friday, sorry, make that 1”, “…, no, earlier”, “…, might be later” → null; before the cue they don't block: “Sorry, I'm sick, leaving at noon today” is 5), AND a day named after the cue sits in the cue's own clause (“I'm sick, leaving at noon, and out tomorrow too” → null), AND the message refers to exactly ONE day (today incl. tonight/this morning/this afternoon, tomorrow, yesterday, weekdays and dates, each resolved — “Friday, Nov 6” is one day; “Out sick this afternoon and tomorrow” → full days) and the absence is that single day. “this morning”, “this afternoon”, “tonight” are today in the date reader (“Out sick this afternoon and tomorrow” → today and tomorrow, full days). “back/return(ing) <day>” is the return day: any message with one gives NO dates (HR asks) unless it is exactly “<one day>, back <the next working day>” (“Out Friday, back Monday” → Friday), with the back day resolved for real and nothing else in its phrase (“back next Thursday”, “back Friday next week”, “back Monday the 16th” → no dates). “back” counts only when DIRECTLY followed by [on] [next|this] and a day or date — “My back hurts and I need Friday off” is Friday (an open Friday card keeps its dates); “Out Wednesday, back Friday”, “I'm sick today, back Wednesday”, “Sick, going home at 1pm, back Wednesday” → no dates — in follow-ups too (the card's dates are cleared). A change of day said as “X to Y” / “from X to Y” / “Y instead of X” together with a change word (change, move, switch, swap, shift, instead, not, rather than) ANYWHERE in the message is never read as a range (“I need time off. Please change it, Friday to Monday”, “Not Friday, Monday to Wednesday”; a per-clause version was reverted — “conference Monday to Wednesday, not vacation” also makes HR ask): no dates (HR asks), in a first message and in a follow-up (“Change Friday to Monday” on a Thursday or a next-Friday card clears the dates). An arrival partial (“coming in at 10am”, “2 hours late”) carries `side: 'arrive'` and the card says “until 10:00 (2 hours)”; leaving stays “from 15:00 (2 hours)”. Follow-ups are merged fail safe. DATES: an addition word (also, plus, as well, too, add, another, extra) with any day clears them (HR asks “Which day or days?”) — never extend, never replace; else a follow-up that names one of the card's CURRENT days together with other days clears them (“Move Friday to Monday and Tuesday”, “Change Friday to Monday and Tuesday”, “Not Friday, Monday and Tuesday”, “Switch it from Friday to Monday”, “Make it Thursday and Friday” on a Friday card — the date reader would read “Friday to Monday” as a range); else dates read cleanly replace them (“Change it to Monday and Tuesday” → Mon–Tue); else a day that couldn't be read (“Actually Monday, not Friday”) clears them; else they stay. HOURS: a real hour signal (a clock time; hour(s)/hr(s); early/late; morning/afternoon/evening/tonight; half day; full/all/whole/entire day; any number or number word not in a date) uses its one clear value or else a full day — never the old value (“Actually make it a full day”, “No, I will be out all day”, “Make it three instead”, “make that 1pm”); correction words alone keep the hours (“No note needed”, “Make it a personal holiday instead”). (A first version extended “also Monday” into a Fri–Mon range and gave confident wrong dates; removed.) Otherwise null: a full day the professor edits. History: “Off Nov 6, 4 hours” once became 8 h; word-list patches kept giving wrong hours (“afternoon = 4” after a rejected early/late phrase, “meeting at 3pm” read as leaving at 3pm), so the rule became structural and strict.
- **Every model call must reach Topology, and only the monitor.** The Professor's view model calls (staff classifier, naming a direct post, the @assistant) emitted with no run, and the UI server drops run-less specialist events that aren't configuration events, so the Professor's model box said “No calls yet”. Emit them in the Professor view's runs: `staff-<operationId>` (the Hub's staff run; also the card's Submit/Cancel) and `prof-<operationId>` (a chat turn), with no message words. The UI server accepts `staff-*` only from the Professor, the Hub and HR and `prof-*` only from the Professor, with the suffix checked by the operation-ID rule (`lessons.js` `ID`, 6–80 chars), and keeps both out of the student's AG-UI stream (they were nested inside an open student run). Mock and missing-key turns make no call and no hop.
- **The date reader returns null rather than a wrong date.** Anything it can't read fully (a gap, a leftover number) is asked, never guessed.
- **Don't silently shorten a day list.** “Nov 5th and 6th” and “Nov 5, 6 and 7” once read as Nov 5 only. Read every listed day (each the day after the one before) or return null and ask; never drop a day and never widen a gap.
- **The relationship row must always be on the card.** A card made without a reason (“I need Friday off”) had no relationship row, so picking Bereavement/Funeral on the card was a dead end (Submit required a field that wasn't there). Always show the row; make its star and check follow the name picked on the card.
- **Chat follow-ups must not undo card edits.** Re-issuing the card from the old values plus the new words lost what the professor had changed on the card (e.g. dates). Send the card's current values with the follow-up and merge them first, then the words.
- **The mock staff check must fail toward asking.** (Strong leave cues — personal day, day off, PTO, vacation, holiday, not well, unwell, under the weather, out sick, funeral, family emergency, surgery — with I/I'm/I'd/my → staff request, without → ask. Soft cues — late, early, appointment, dentist, doctor — only ever ASK (“I will not accept work turned in late”, “If you have a dentist appointment, tell me in advance”, “I'm coming in 2 hours late Monday” → the unsure question; “HR” then files it), except in a message that starts with a student tag AND has no first-person word (“@alex your homework is late” is to that student and is delivered; “@alex I have a dentist appointment Friday”, “@alex @sam I'll be in late Tuesday” ask). First person + “off <day>” (“I'm off Friday”, “@alex I'm off Friday”) also asks. Holiday class notes are asked about or sent to HR, never posted on a guess — an attempt to post them was reverted.) “Taking Friday off for a funeral”, “Out sick tomorrow” and “Need Friday off, family emergency” have no “I/my”, and the first heuristic posted them to the class. Any leave word without first-person words is `unsure`.
- **Short leave words are private too.** The 5-letter rule missed “sick”, “died” and “flu”, so “the professor is sick” could reach students after “I'm out sick Thursday”. Always add the fixed leave words found in staff messages to the private words.
- **No guessing the reason.** “I need Friday off” leaves the Absence name empty and asks. Never default to Vacation. The “because you said” quote must be in the professor's message.
- **A short, unclear answer is never broadcast to the class.** After the unsure question, “yes”, “huh?” or “neither” must not become a class post. Re-ask once, then drop it (see Rule B below for which replies are a new message).
- **Replies to the unsure question follow one strict structural rule (Rule B) and fail safe.** A reply ending in “?” (“HR or class?”) is always asked again. A reply with ANY choice word (HR, human resources, file, class, students, them, both, tell, post) is never classified on its own: it is acted on only when (a) its FIRST clause (up to . , ; — or “and”) is a pure HR or both choice (one other word allowed) and no later clause picks the class instead (“Please file it with HR, and also I need Monday off” → HR + the not-sent notice), or (b) the whole reply is a pure class choice (posts the held text); a bare refusal drops the hold (a negation inside his own content, “I'm not feeling well”, is not a refusal); anything else (“Just tell the class, HR already knows I'm out Friday”, “Class only. I'll handle the HR side myself on Monday”, “class first, then HR”, a new request that contains “file”) is held with the notice. Only a reply with NO choice word and more than 8 words is classified on its own. A reply of 8 words or fewer is never posted or classified on its own: a negation anywhere drops the hold (“Okay, nothing was filed or posted.”) — read part by part (split at , ; but then), so “HR, not the class” is HR, while “no, not HR”, “not the class”, “don't file it”, “just leave it” drop it. The held text is posted ONLY for a pure class choice with no negation anywhere (“Not HR. Class please” is held) — choice words and filler with no other word (“class”, “the class”, “tell the class”, “students”, “class only”, “post it to the class please, thanks”); “it isn't for the class”, “class shouldn't”, “without the class”, “except the class”, “exclude students” are asked again. HR or both (which never post) may carry one other word (“HR I guess”, “send it to HR asap”, “both, thx”, “class first, then HR” = both, “class too”/“class also” = both — “too”/“also” are not filler). A choice-word reply that is not a clear choice gets ONE neutral holding text, true in every case: “I'm still holding your earlier message — please answer HR, the class, or both. If your reply also had something new, send it again after.” (then dropped on the next unclear reply; “Tell the class, and post that the exam is postponed” → that text, nothing posted; “class” then posts only the held text). Other unclear replies are asked once more (the plain question unless the reply brings new content), then dropped; if that re-asked reply brings NEW content (a day, date or leave word not in the held message — “Also need Monday off, same reason”, “Can I also take next Monday off for a doctor appointment?”) the re-ask says “I'm still holding your earlier message — answer HR, the class, or both. Your new message wasn't sent; please send it again after.” A reply of more than 8 words: a clear choice acts the same way with no model call; otherwise it is classified on its own first — a staff request goes to HR with its OWN words and the held message is dropped (“not feeling well” never drops a real new request); a class message that mentions HR/file/class/students/them/both/tell is asked once more; any other class message is NOT posted on that one verdict — the professor is asked “Should I post this to the class: “<reply>”? Reply yes or no.” and it is posted (as his own message) only after a clear yes: negation → “Okay, I won't post it.”; a reply naming HR/human resources/file is never a yes (“yes, to HR”, “Please send it to HR” → not posted); a yes with any other word than yes/yeah/sure/ok/okay/post it/please/go ahead/thanks is asked once more (the same holds for the neutral HR note offer); unsure asks the question about this message. **One R3 rule for every pending question** (the held HR/class/both question, the class-post offer, the note offer): a reply that brings NEW content — a day, date, month, number or leave word not in the pending text, compared as normalized tokens (“friday's” = “Friday”; “just leave it” is not a leave word) — is never dropped silently: the clear answer is acted on (yes / no / HR / class / both) and the professor is always told “Your new message wasn't sent; please send it again.” (in the reply for a clarify, otherwise as its own assistant chat entry; his message is kept private), or, for the held question with no clear answer, “I'm still holding your earlier message …”. With new content the first sentence decides a yes (“Yes please. Also I need Monday off …” is a yes; “Yes, and I also need Tuesday off” is asked again). A reply over 8 words that explicitly names HR or both AND has new content (“Please file it with HR, and also I need Monday off”, “Both please, and I'll also be out next Monday …”) acts on the held message without being classified; one that only mentions the class never posts the held text (a pure class choice is required). History: a new full request was once filed as the held text; “please file it with HR for me…” was posted verbatim to students; “not the class” and “it isn't for the class” posted the held leave text; a first Rule B draft dropped a real new request.
- **Negation beats yes.** “ok, but don't tell them”, “please don't post it” and “sure, no actually” are refusals. Check the negation before the yes.
- **A card must not live forever.** The desk's open card needs its own timer (`HUB_FORM_TTL_MS`), or it swallows class messages after the Hub already expired it. Close the card on the typed `card_closed` code. Never decide a card is closed by matching refusal wording.
- **New conversation closes open cards.** Both the HR reset and the classroom reset must close every open card (`formHost.closeAll`), or an old card can be submitted into the fresh store. The Hub also drops its formRefs on a classroom reset.
- **Drop a stale relationship.** If the absence changes from Bereavement to Vacation, the old “grandmother” must not stay on the card.
- **A2UI ChoicePicker has no dropdown.** The v0.9 basic catalog's `displayStyle` is only `checkbox` or `chips`. Don't promise a select. Use chips (all ten names wrap onto three lines).
- **Validate partial hours.** `plan` must reject partial hours that are zero, negative, not a number, or more than the standard day. The card's hours must map to the store exactly as in section 5.
- **Cap the range.** 400 days maximum, as a clear `too_long` refusal, not a silent cut. A loop over a range must be bounded.
- **Validators need limits and unknown-key rejection.** Reject unknown keys on the card's values. Limit the relationship (100) and note (1000), trimmed, text only. Guard null and arrays in the key check. Keep the store's operations in a `Map`, not a plain object (an operation ID such as `__proto__` is hostile).
- **Sign the conversation too.** A staff action signature that covers only the operation and formRef can be replayed into another conversation. Include `conversationId` in the signed fields and make it required.
- **HR must not be reachable by students.** Staff destinations stay off the public card, out of the student router and out of `requestedCapability`. HR re-checks the Hub's staff signature even when forced. Test both.
- **Don't write HR records anywhere.** Same rule as the loans: memory only. Tests must never touch `data/`.
- **Don't leak through telemetry.** Staff words, absence name, relationship and note must not appear in staff-run events. A test that searches the whole stream finds HR's own public skill example text (“My grandmother passed away…”), which is skill config, not the professor's words: search the staff-run events only.
- **Keep the harness honest.** A test Hub must not follow a real HR on 41245. Pre-pick the HR port and pass `HR_URL`.
- **Dates in tests depend on today.** A “this Thursday and Friday” test can only check the title and relationship. Submit tests use fixed dates in the action's context.
- **Known limits to say honestly:** the “when” line and balance bar on the card are HR's summary from when the card was made; a refused Submit shows once in the card's notice and once as an HR bubble; “going home at noon” counts 5 hours (a partial day ends at 17:00); red-star and `display: contents` CSS depend on the pinned renderer's light-DOM structure (`@a2ui/lit` 0.14.0), so re-run `hr-card.js` after an upgrade.
