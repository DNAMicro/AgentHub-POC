    What the research found, and how sure we are

    Three research agents looked at monitoring tools, games and visualization research. All three rated their own results “moderate”: the patterns below were confirmed from the linked pages, but no product publishes its exact dimming level or animation timing, so those numbers in this mockup are my own starting points to tune by eye.

    • Light the path a request took, leave the rest on the map. AWS Step Functions colours each step by what happened to it in one run (AWS docs). Closest match to our fixed diagram.
    • Grey means “idle”, and it is a state of its own. Kiali draws links that had traffic earlier but none now in grey, and can hide them (Kiali docs).
    • Motion only where something is happening. Kiali moves dots only along links with traffic (Kiali docs); Heer and Robertson (2007) measured that animated changes help people follow objects (paper).
    • Hover or select to spotlight one part. Datadog's Service Map highlights a service and isolates it with its neighbours (Datadog docs).
    • Three states, not two. Fog of war in strategy games: hidden, seen before but dim, visible now (Age of Empires).
    • One system at a time. Oxygen Not Included has about 16 overlays (wiki); Cities: Skylines has 19 info views, some opening by themselves with the matching tool (dev diary summary).
    • Do not make highlighting a switch the audience must press. The Witcher 3's makers said its highlight mode was overused (OpenCritic).
    • Keep every part in the same place. Studies on moving layouts disagree, but a stable layout is the safe choice for people's memory of the picture (survey).
    • Dimmed outlines still need 3:1 contrast. WCAG 1.4.11 (W3C). That is why idle parts here are drawn in a solid grey, not faded with transparency.

    Not confirmed by any source found: Tufte's and Shneiderman's rules (recalled, not looked up), the “about four moving things” tracking limit, and anything about Factorio, Paradox map modes, EVE, LangGraph Studio, Temporal or transit maps.
