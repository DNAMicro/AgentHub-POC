Topology: Canvas joins as a system, not an agent

Same family as PeopleSoft (design F): a rounded tile, its own icon (a quiz clipboard), sparkle and sweep while busy, the dash-dot MCP line and a hexagon pulse. No circle, no model box.

Each system sits in the right-hand column under its agent’s AI model box: Canvas under Faculty’s model, PeopleSoft under HR’s model. The model box moves up a little; the agent’s HTTPS line goes up-right to its model and its MCP line goes down-right to its system. Only Faculty talks to Canvas; only HR talks to PeopleSoft.

Shown mid-call: “Working…”, hexagon on its way to Canvas. Colour: moss green (new; not used by any other node).
