Absence name and reason — dropdown design (Option 1)

Two dropdowns: Absence name (34 names), then Reason (only that name's reasons). Closed, each shows just its value with a ▾. Click it: a search box and a scrolling list open; type to narrow, click to pick, and it closes (Esc or clicking outside closes too). Under the fields, each square is one 8-hour day of leave left, in groups of 7; striped squares are this request. Sample person: Professor Amir, department chair on a 12-month appointment (earns vacation). Changing the name clears a reason that doesn't belong; a name with one reason, or a reason worded like the name (Vacation → Vacation), fills it in. Left: HR filled everything. Right: no reason was given, so the name is empty and highlighted and HR asks in the chat. Real list from “Absence Type and Reasons.xlsx”; sample data, made up.
