AgentHub · Marketing 351 demo

What you can do, and what to say

AgentHub is a small, fictional university run by AI agents. You talk to it in plain words. This guide lists what each side can do, grouped by task, with phrases you can copy and try.

1Open the app

With the demo running, go to http://127.0.0.1:4444 in your browser.

2Pick your side

The Conversation tab is you as the student Alex Student. The Faculty view tab is you as the instructor, Dr. Dabirian.

3Type, don't click

Write in the box at the bottom and press Enter. There are no menus: you ask, and the right agent answers.

4Start over any time

New conversation (under the student chat) clears both screens and resets the class, loans and records.

Everything here is fictional. The students, emails, grades, HR, Canvas and PeopleSoft systems are demo stand-ins. No real email is sent and nothing is booked. Answers come from AI models, so the wording can differ a little from this guide.

S

Student Conversation tab

You are Alex Student. Your Student agent sends your words to the university, which finds the right service: the Marketing 351 course assistant, the Tutor, or the Library.

Marketing questions

Dr. Dabirian's course assistant explains course ideas using the course notes.

  • What are the 4 Ps of marketing?An explanation with an example
  • Can you explain price elasticity simply

Class facts

When and where the class meets, exams, topics, office hours and the syllabus, answered straight away.

  • When does Marketing 351 meet?Tue & Thu, 10:00–11:15 AM in Mihaylo Hall 2405
  • When is the final exam for Marketing 351?
  • What topic are we covering next in marketing?
  • What are Dr. Dabirian's office hours?
  • Can I get the Marketing 351 syllabus?A summary of the units, exams and grading
  • Is my marketing class cancelled on Thursday?Checks the cancellations Dr. Dabirian sent you
  • When is the marketing quiz due?

Grades live in Canvas: the assistant will tell you so rather than guess.

Classmates

Who is in your class, with their year, major and campus email so you can contact them.

  • Who is in my classAll 24 classmates with year, major and email
  • How many students are in my marketing class?
  • Is Mariana Aguilar in my class?
  • What's Sam Student's email?
  • Who are the seniors in my marketing class?
  • Can I get the emails of the juniors in my marketing class?

Private: student IDs, phone numbers, pronouns and grades of other students are never shared.

Writing to Dr. Dabirian

Address your message to Dr. Dabirian and it goes straight to them. Simple facts are answered for you instead.

  • Hi Dr. Dabirian can I turn in my homework late“Sent to Dr. Dabirian — they’ll get back to you here.”
  • Can you ask Dr. Dabirian if I can bring a laptop?Sent at once
  • Can I get an extension on the homework?“That’s Dr. Dabirian’s call. Want me to send your question to them?” — answer yes or no
  • Hi Dr. Dabirian, when is the final?A simple fact, so the assistant answers it

Nothing is sent on a guess. When the assistant offers to pass a question on, it waits for your yes (for 15 minutes).

Messages from Dr. Dabirian

Messages, class cancellations and quiz notices appear in your chat. Lessons appear in your inbox.

  • ↩ Reply (button under a message from Dr. Dabirian)Your reply goes to Dr. Dabirian’s chat
  • What did Dr. Dabirian say in the last message?

Quiz notices are information only: take the quiz in Canvas.

Library: loans and renewals

See the books you have out and renew one. The renewal screen is built for you on the spot.

  • When is the library open?It may ask which day you mean

Fill a form by chatting

While a form is open, type a value in the chat and your Student agent puts it in the form. You still press the form’s button yourself.

  • december 1With the renewal form open: fills the new return date (if the date is allowed)

Not available to students

Some services belong to staff only, and some questions are outside the university’s services.

  • Can you tell everyone in my marketing class that the study group meets tonight?Only Dr. Dabirian can message the whole class; it offers to pass it on
F

Faculty Faculty view tab

You are Dr. Dabirian, teaching Marketing 351 (25 students). Write to your class, or ask your course assistant. It also reaches HR, Canvas and the class roster for you.

Message your class

Every message opens a preview card first: the exact text and who gets it. Nothing goes out until you press Send.

  • Reminder: the case study write-up is due Friday at noon.Your exact words, to all 25 students
  • notify everyone that office hours move to 3 PM on Wednesday
  • tell everyone to read chapter 5 before Tuesday“Please read chapter 5 before Tuesday.”
  • notify everyone about the midterm reviewThe card asks what to say about it

On the card: edit the text, press Send or Don’t send, or simply type yes / no. You then see “25 students notified.”

Message some students

Tag students with @, or name a group by year or major. The list is read fresh from the class roster.

  • @mariana-aguilar please see me about your project outlineTo Mariana Aguilar only
  • message the marketing majors to bring laptops on ThursdayTo your 15 Marketing majors
  • send a message to all seniors to inform them of a mandatory lab workTo your 10 seniors, with a drafted message for you to check
  • message Alex and Sam about the study group

Tip: type @ to get name suggestions. Tags look like @first-last.

Class roster

See who is enrolled and ask questions about your class. Records come from the student records system (IDs are masked).

  • show me my class rosterA roster card: name, masked ID, year, program and plan, units
  • how many seniors in my class“10 of your 25 students are seniors.”
  • who are my seniors?They are highlighted on the roster
  • how many marketing majors do I have?
  • Is Sam Student in my class?
  • list students with fewer than 12 units

Look only: the roster can’t be emailed or exported, and every lookup is logged.

Time off (HR)

Say what you need. HR answers with a filled-in card: check it and press Submit.

  • I would like to take vacation on Thursday Nov 12A Vacation card for that day → Submit → “Your time off is recorded.”
  • doctor appointment Tuesday morningA half day (4 hours)
  • jury duty Nov 19
  • make it 4 hours insteadWith a card open: changes the card
  • how much vacation do I have left?
  • what time off have I requested?

Missing class? If the days you file include a class day, a cancellation card appears with the notice your students would get. Add an optional note (for example a makeup plan), then Send to students or Don’t send. It never mentions why you are out.

Quizzes (Canvas)

Your assistant drafts a quiz from a topic you have already taught. You edit it, save it in Canvas, then publish.

  • make a quizButtons for the topics you’ve taught: tap one
  • make a 6 question quiz on topic 8A draft card; each question tests a different idea

On the card: edit questions or settings, Draft one more, then Approve (saved in Canvas, students can’t see it yet) and Publish. Students get a quiz notice.

Course progress and your assistant

Ask where the class is in the course, or start a message with @assistant to ask for help.

  • what topics have we covered so far?
  • what's the next topic?
  • @assistant write a short reminder to the class that the group project proposal is due next MondayA drafted message on a preview card
  • @assistant what is the difference between segmentation and targeting?

Answer your students

  • ↩ Reply (button on the student’s card)Your answer opens a preview card addressed to that student
