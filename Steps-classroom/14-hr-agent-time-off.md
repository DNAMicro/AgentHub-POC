# Step 14 — HR Agent: staff time off from the Professor chat

Time: about 120 minutes. Cost: free (tests use mocks). Optional live check: PAID, a few cents.

(The number is 14 because 12 and 13 are reserved by the guardrails design.)

## What you'll see when it works

- In **Professor view**, type “My grandmother passed away, I need this Thursday and Friday.” HR answers with one card that opens with a shaded summary you can read in 3 seconds: **Bereavement**, the reason under it (**Grandparent/Great Grandparent**), the dates and length in words, and “This doesn't use your vacation or sick leave.” Below it are two autocomplete dropdowns (Absence name and Reason), the dates and hours, an optional note with a small ⓘ privacy notice, and Submit request. You press **Submit request** and the chat says “Sent to your manager for a decision.” with a request number.
- Type “I need Friday off.” The card appears with the **Absence name dropdown empty and highlighted**, and HR's lead line asks what kind of time off it is. Answer “it's a vacation day” in the chat, or pick it in the card. Type “what's my sick balance?” and the answer comes in the chat, not on the card.
- Type “I'm sick, cancel Friday's class.” The app asks “Should I file this as time off with HR, tell the class, or both?” and does nothing else until you answer.
- Type “Read chapter 4 before Friday.” It is posted to the class exactly as before.
- Topology shows a fourth specialist, HR Agent, “Staff only”. A student can never reach it.

### Task A — Form, store, readers

1. **Form (`buildTimeOffForm`):** a Column. Row “Kind of leave”: two ChoicePickers, both `filterable: true` and `displayStyle: 'chips'` (single choice; the browser draws them as closed autocomplete dropdowns — the v0.9 catalog has no select, and the agent sends a standard component), `absenceType` (names) and `absenceReason` (`reasonPicker(type)`: only that name's reasons). Row “Dates and hours”: DateTimeInput `beginDate`, “to”, `endDate` (date only, **no min/max**), TextField `hours`. TextField `note` labelled “Note for your manager” (optional), then buttons “Submit request” and “Cancel”. Submit's check needs both pickers non-empty. The date and hours labels end with “ *” (“Begin date *”, “End date *”, “Hours *”); the two pickers are labelled “Absence name” and “Reason” without it (the browser's CSS adds a red “*” after the picker labels). Data paths `/absenceType`, `/absenceReason`, `/beginDate`, `/endDate`, `/hours`, `/note` plus the `/ui` flags the other forms use. There is no relationship field: for Bereavement the reason is the relative. The approved mockup is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-hr-card-redesign.html`. Allowed set: Column, Row, Text, ChoicePicker, DateTimeInput, TextField, Button; functions required, regex, length, and, not, or.
2. **`validateTimeOff`:** reject unknown keys first (guard null and arrays). Name on the list and name + reason a real pair. Real dates (guard `Date` errors), begin <= end. Hours a plain number more than 0 and at most 24 per day, and fitting the dates (`These hours don’t fit the dates.`). `note` at most 1000 characters after trimming; non-strings are errors.
   **`checkTimeOffValues(values)`** (the open card's current values sent with a chat follow-up): non-object or unknown keys → `{ ok: false }`; otherwise each field is checked with the same field rules and kept if valid and non-empty, dropped if not (a reason that does not belong to the chosen name is dropped) → `{ ok, values, errors }`.
3. **Store:** staff `staff-amir` “Professor Amir”, a department chair on a 12-month appointment: standard day 8, Vacation 96 (earns 16 h a month), Sick 120 (8 h a month), Personal Holiday 8 (one day a year). `HOLIDAYS = ['2026-11-11', '2026-11-26', '2026-11-27', '2026-12-25']` (in the shared module). Workdays are Monday to Friday minus holidays. `plan`: unknown staff; unknown type or pair (validate the type first); range over **400 days** → `too_long`; begin or end on a holiday → `holiday_boundary`; no workdays → `no_workdays`; partial hours not finite, not more than 0 or not less than the standard day → `bad_hours`; partial modes `start_day_only` (first day shortened) and `all_days` (the same hours each day); no credit type → ok; more than the balance → `not_enough` with “Not enough sick leave: 16 hours needed, 8 left.”; else ok with `credit: { type, now, after }`. Bound every loop.
4. **`submit`:** synchronous (no `await` between plan and write). Operations in a `Map` keyed by operation ID, storing the request fingerprint: same values → the stored result, different values → `operation_reused`. Receipt `{ requestId: 'TO-<up to 8 hex of the id>', absenceType, absenceReason, absenceName, reasonText, beginDate, endDate, hours, status: 'sent_to_manager' }`. Nothing is deducted. `reset()` reseeds everything. `balances()` and `requests()` (newest first, receipt fields) feed the chat answers.
5. **`readDates(text, now)`** (code only): weekdays (“this Thursday and Friday” — a day in a list is the next one after the one before it; “next Monday” is in the following Monday-start week; “last”), today/tomorrow/yesterday, “Nov 5”, “Nov 5–7”, “November 5th”, “11/5”, “11/5/2026”, and a month with a day list (“Nov 5th and 6th” → Nov 5–6, “Nov 5, 6 and 7” → Nov 5–7; each day must follow the one before, so “Nov 5 and 7” → null; a joiner and number left over after a month date, like “Nov 5th and the 6th” → null). Rules: skip fractions (“1/2”, “3/4”) and numbers followed by day/hour words; join mentions only with “and”, a comma, “to”, “through”, “until” or a dash; a cross-month range (“Dec 30 – 2”) ends in the next month, never earlier than the start; a month and day with no year is this year unless more than 180 days past; **anything it can't read with confidence returns null, never a wrong date**. The partial-day reader understands “leaving at noon”, “from 2pm”, “this afternoon” and gives `{ mode: 'start_day_only', hours }` where a partial day ends at 17:00.
6. **`chooseAbsence`:** schema `{ pair (enum of the 51 TYPE:CODE keys + ''), quote, note }`. Live: one `callModel` (400 tokens, no sampling params), system text lists each pair as `TYPE:CODE = Name — Reason` and says: pick exactly one pair or “” when the words don't say why; never guess from the day; copy the quote exactly; never invent dates, hours, names or reasons; for a death or a sick relative just pick the type (code chooses the reason). Mock: a labelled heuristic proposing a pair (death words → Bereavement; sick words → Sick Leave; jury; conference/workshop/training; personal holiday; vacation/trip). `verify()`: keep the type only if it exists **and** the quote is in the professor's words (case-insensitive); Bereavement reason = `reasonForRelative` (a fixed relative → reason map, longest word wins, two different relatives → empty); Sick Leave becomes Family Care only via `familyCare` (the relative is the one ill or cared for by the speaker); other types take the model's code if it is a real pair, else `defaultReason`. Cap the note at 300 characters. Otherwise return an empty name. A live failure returns an empty name and a sanitized code. **Part days from hours:** `spreadOf(days, hours, wordsMode)` in the shared module (H = 8N full; N = 1 one day; first-day-partial fits when 8(N−1) < H < 8N; same-hours-each-day fits when H/N < 8 and is a whole number of quarter hours; both fit → the words decide or HR asks).
7. **Tests:** every rule above, including the problems listed below.

### Task C — The Professor's staff desk

1. **`classifyStaff({ text, emit })`** → `{ kind, model }` with kind `staff_request | class_message | unsure`. Live: one structured call. A failure or missing key → `unsure`. Mock: a labelled heuristic that fails toward asking: first-person + leave pattern → staff (plus class words → unsure); any other leave word (time off, day off, sick, ill, funeral, passed away, bereavement, vacation, leave, family emergency, doctor, hospital) without “I/my” → `unsure`, never `class_message`. Staff means the professor asks for himself (“I need Friday off”, “I'm taking vacation next week”). “Please leave your essays in my mailbox” is a class message. “I will be out Monday, no class” is unsure.
2. **`staffTurn`** runs first in `professorChat`, unless the message has `@assistant` or is a reply to one student (they skip the desk). Memory-only state: `conversationId` (new after a reset), `openCard { formRef, a2ui, hrCard, conversationId, alsoTellClass, at }`, `pendingChoice` (the held message), `pendingNote`.
3. **`unsure`:** ask exactly “Should I file this as time off with HR, tell the class, or both?” and hold the message. Answers are read by code: “both”, or HR words together with class words → HR gets the held message, `alsoTellClass`; HR words (`hr|file|filed|filing|time off|leave|staff`) → HR; class words (`class|students|everyone|tell`) → the held message is posted as today; a negation → “OK — nothing was filed with HR or posted to the class.”; a short reply (4 words or fewer) that is none of these → the same question once more, then the same “nothing was filed” text; a longer reply → a new message. A held question is forgotten after 15 minutes.
4. **`staff_request`** → `hubLink.sendSigned(STAFF_REQUEST_MEDIA_TYPE, …, { timeoutMs: 60000 })` with the conversationId. While a card is open, every plain message goes to HR as a follow-up (with `formValues` when the browser sent them for that same `formRef`) and the open card is replaced by the answer.
5. **Chat entries:** HR text, receipt and failures are `role: 'hr'`; the question and note offer are `assistant` entries. Every staff-turn entry is saved with `staff: true` (a held message later sent to the class goes back to `staff: false`). Never store a2ui or the card.
6. **Submit (accepted):** `hr` entry “Sent to your manager for a decision. Request TO-…: <name>, <Day, Mon D>[ – <Day, Mon D>], <hours> h.” and clear the card. If marked “both”, add an `assistant` entry: “Want me to tell the class: “Professor Amir is out on Fri, Nov 6.”? Reply yes to post it.” (built by code from the receipt dates; a range reads “… is out on Thu, Nov 5 – Fri, Nov 6.”). “yes” posts that exact text to `all` with `verbatim: false`; any negation anywhere → “Okay, I won’t post it.”; a short unclear reply → “Should I post that note to the class? Reply yes or no.” once, then decline. Check negation before yes.
7. **Closing the card:** on a Submit result, Cancel, reset, the typed `card_closed` code, or its own timer (`HUB_FORM_TTL_MS`, default 2 h). Never close by matching wording. A refused Submit keeps the card open.
8. **Routes:** `POST /author/hr-action` (origin-guarded, one at a time with the chat, deduped by `chatOps['hr:'+op]` for definite outcomes only). `GET /author/state` adds `hrCard: { formRef, a2ui, card } | null`. `/author/reset` calls `resetStaff()`. `/author/chat` accepts optional `formRef` + `formValues` (`cardValues` in `src/staff.js`; malformed → 400). The UI server checks them the same way on `POST /api/professor/chat` and on AG-UI professor runs (`forwardedProps`). UI server `POST /api/professor/hr/actions` `{ operationId, formRef, action }` (strict: no extra keys, id formats, only the workflow's own button and context keys via `checkAction`, `validateFinal` on Submit) forwards through `professorCall('/hr-action')` and the Hub's `POST /dev/agents/:agentId/author/hr-action` (origin-guarded), then refreshes the AG-UI state. `relayEvent` lets `staff-<operationId>` runs through; `CONFIG_EVENTS` gains `staff_decision`.
9. **Privacy:** in `assistantTurn`, replace every `staff: true` entry with the marker `[private: the professor’s own HR matter — never post, quote or repeat it to students]`, and tell the model never to post, quote or hint at it. In `checkPost`, count only non-staff professor messages as his words; refuse a draft (code `private_staff`) that uses a distinctive word (5 or more letters, not common) **or a fixed leave word of any length** (sick, ill, died, flu, leave, funeral, passed, death, grief, surgery, doctor, hospital) from his staff messages unless the current `@assistant` request contains that word.

## Avoid these known problems

- **Read “half a day” as 4 hours.** “half a day”, “a half day”, “half of the day” are the same cue as “half day”; reading only “half day” left “for half a day” at a full 8 hours (user report 2026-11-03: “take my kid to school next Tuesday for half a day” showed 8).
- **Never pick a name for a school visit, and never leave him with only the empty dropdown.** No absence name covers it; California Labor Code 230.8 has school activities use vacation or personal time, otherwise unpaid, so HR offers Vacation, Personal Holiday and Dock as choices (only for his own child's school, not when the child is ill: that is Family Care).
- **Don't shadow the shared `nameOf` in the browser.** `public/classroom.js` has its own `nameOf` for student names; import the absence one as `absenceName`, or every absence code looks like a real name.
- The `@assistant` model must never see the professor's HR messages (private marker) and `checkPost` must refuse drafts that reuse their words (`private_staff`).
- Don't read fractions (“1/2 day”) as dates; don't merge unrelated dates; a cross-month range never ends before it starts; return null rather than a wrong date: “Nov 5th and 6th” / “Nov 5, 6 and 7” read every day or ask, never Nov 5 only and never a widened gap.
- A number followed by hours/a time is never a day: “Nov 6 - 2 hrs” and “Nov 6, 4 hours” mean Nov 6 (a range end or leftover day must not be followed by hrs/hours/am/pm/a time).
- Rule A (partial days, strict, fail safe): a clock time counts only right after his own leaving verb (→ 17:00 − T) or arrival verb (→ T − 08:00; not “starting at”, “from”, “back at”), and any other clock time in the message blocks (“meeting at 3pm”, “starting at 2pm”, “no wait, noon”); morning/afternoon/half day count only when tied to off/out/sick/leaving; plus “N hours”, “N-hour”, “N hours early/late”. Drop 8+/over-24, “notice”, “in N hours”, “of meetings”; an early/late phrase that isn't his own blocks. A partial day only when exactly one readable cue is left, no correction/alternative marker (or, make it, otherwise, unless, instead, I mean, actually, perhaps, maybe, either, jk, nvm, never mind, scratch that, on second thought, cancel, no wait, correction), no number/number word (dates excepted) or retraction/doubt word (sorry, moved, oops, make that, no, wait, earlier, later, possibly, maybe, might, probably) after the one cue, a later day only in the cue's own clause, exactly one day referred to, and the absence is one day; “this morning/afternoon”, “tonight” = today; any “back/return <day>” → no dates unless exactly “<one day>, back <next working day>” (back day resolved for real, nothing else in its phrase; “back” only when directly followed by a day — “My back hurts and I need Friday off” is Friday); a change word anywhere with “X to Y” days (“Change Friday to Monday”) → no dates, first message or follow-up; arrival partials show “until 10:00”; follow-ups: an addition word + a day clears the dates, naming the card's current day with other days clears them (“Move Friday to Monday and Tuesday”), clean dates replace them, an unreadable day clears them, else they stay; a real hour signal uses its one clear value or a full day (never the old hours), correction words alone keep them; otherwise null (full day, he edits).
