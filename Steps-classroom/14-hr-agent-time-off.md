# Step 14 — HR Agent: staff time off from the Professor chat

Time: about 120 minutes. Cost: free (tests use mocks). Optional live check: PAID, a few cents.

(The number is 14 because 12 and 13 are reserved by the guardrails design.)

## What you'll see when it works

- In **Professor view**, type “My grandmother passed away, I need this Thursday and Friday.” HR answers with one card: **Bereavement/Funeral**, the two dates, 16 hours, relationship “grandmother”, and “Chosen because you said “grandmother passed away”.” You press **Submit request** and the chat says “Sent to your manager for a decision.” with a request number.
- Type “I need Friday off.” The card appears with the **Absence name empty and highlighted**, and HR asks in the chat what kind of time off it is. Answer “it's a vacation day” in the chat, or click a chip in the card.
- Type “I'm sick, cancel Friday's class.” The app asks “Should I file this as time off with HR, tell the class, or both?” and does nothing else until you answer.
- Type “Read chapter 4 before Friday.” It is posted to the class exactly as before.
- Topology shows a fourth specialist, HR Agent, “Staff only”. A student can never reach it.

### Task A — Form, store, readers

1. **Form (`buildTimeOffForm`):** Column of Rows, one field per row: ChoicePicker `absenceName` with `displayStyle: 'chips'` (the v0.9 catalog has no dropdown), DateTimeInput `beginDate` and `endDate` (date only, **no min/max**), TextField `hours`, TextField `relationship` (**always on the card**; label bound to `/ui/relationshipLabel` — `RELATIONSHIP_LABEL.required` “Relationship *” when the name needs one, else `RELATIONSHIP_LABEL.optional` “Relationship (bereavement or family care)”; its check passes when filled or when `/ui/relationshipNeeded` is `''`), TextField `note` labelled “Note for manager” (optional), then buttons “Submit request” and “Cancel”. Required labels end with “ *”. Data paths `/absenceName`, `/beginDate`, `/endDate`, `/hours`, `/relationship`, `/note` plus the `/ui` flags the other forms use and `/ui/relationshipNeeded`, `/ui/relationshipLabel`. The approved mockup is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-hr-card-mockup.html`. Allowed set: Column, Row, Text, ChoicePicker, DateTimeInput, TextField, Button; functions required, regex, length, not, or.
2. **`validateTimeOff`:** reject unknown keys first (guard null and arrays). Name on the list. Real dates (guard `Date` errors), begin <= end. Hours a plain number more than 0 and at most 24 per day. Relationship required where applicable. `relationship` at most 100 and `note` at most 1000 characters after trimming; non-strings are errors.
   **`checkTimeOffValues(values)`** (the open card's current values sent with a chat follow-up): non-object or unknown keys → `{ ok: false }`; otherwise each field is checked with the same field rules and kept if valid and non-empty, dropped if not → `{ ok, values, errors }`.
3. **Store:** staff `staff-amir` “Professor Amir”, standard day 8, Vacation 96, Sick 120, Personal Holiday 8. `HOLIDAYS = ['2026-11-11', '2026-11-26', '2026-11-27', '2026-12-25']`. Workdays are Monday to Friday minus holidays. `plan`: unknown staff; range over **400 days** → `too_long`; begin or end on a holiday → `holiday_boundary`; no workdays → `no_workdays`; partial hours not finite, not more than 0 or above the standard day → `bad_hours`; hours = first day (partial or standard) + (days − 1) × standard; no credit type → ok; more than the balance → `not_enough` with “Not enough vacation hours: 104 needed, 96 left.”; else ok with `credit: { type, now, after }`. Bound every loop.
4. **`submit`:** synchronous (no `await` between plan and write). Operations in a `Map` keyed by operation ID, storing the request fingerprint: same values → the stored result, different values → `operation_reused`. Receipt `{ requestId: 'TO-<up to 8 hex of the id>', absenceName, beginDate, endDate, hours, status: 'sent_to_manager' }`. Nothing is deducted. `reset()` reseeds everything.
5. **`readDates(text, now)`** (code only): weekdays (“this Thursday and Friday” — a day in a list is the next one after the one before it; “next Monday” is in the following Monday-start week; “last”), today/tomorrow/yesterday, “Nov 5”, “Nov 5–7”, “November 5th”, “11/5”, “11/5/2026”, and a month with a day list (“Nov 5th and 6th” → Nov 5–6, “Nov 5, 6 and 7” → Nov 5–7; each day must follow the one before, so “Nov 5 and 7” → null; a joiner and number left over after a month date, like “Nov 5th and the 6th” → null). Rules: skip fractions (“1/2”, “3/4”) and numbers followed by day/hour words; join mentions only with “and”, a comma, “to”, “through”, “until” or a dash; a cross-month range (“Dec 30 – 2”) ends in the next month, never earlier than the start; a month and day with no year is this year unless more than 180 days past; **anything it can't read with confidence returns null, never a wrong date**. The partial-day reader understands “leaving at noon”, “from 2pm”, “this afternoon” and gives `{ mode: 'start_day_only', hours }` where a partial day ends at 17:00.
6. **`chooseAbsence`:** schema `{ absence_name (enum ABSENCE_NAMES + ''), quote, relationship, note }`. Live: one `callModel` (400 tokens, no sampling params), system text says: pick exactly one name or “” when the words don't say why; never guess from the day; copy the quote exactly; never invent dates, hours, names or reasons; use `PEOPLE_RULE`. Mock: a labelled heuristic (death words → Bereavement/Funeral with the relative as relationship; relative + sick words → Sick – Family Care; sick words → Sick – Self; jury; conference/workshop/training; personal holiday; vacation/trip). `verify()`: keep the name only if it is on the list **and** the quote is in the professor's words (case-insensitive); keep the relationship only if it is in his words; cap the note at 300 characters. Otherwise return an empty name. A live failure returns an empty name and a sanitized code.
7. **Tests:** every rule above, including the problems listed below.

## Rules that apply to the whole step

- The model proposes, code decides: the absence name only from the list; the quote and relationship only from the professor's words; dates and hours only from code.
- HR serves staff only. Students never reach it.
- Nothing is posted or filed on a guess. Leave words never reach students except the neutral note after “yes”.
- Everything HR knows is in memory only. Never write `data/`.
- No manager agent. The receipt says it was sent, and that is all.

## Avoid these known problems

- The `@assistant` model must never see the professor's HR messages (private marker) and `checkPost` must refuse drafts that reuse their words (`private_staff`).
